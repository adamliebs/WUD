WUD

What U Doing?

A friends-only spontaneous-plans app. Post what you're doing in the next few hours, see what your friends and friends-of-friends are doing, and tap to roll with them.

Features

  • Animated feed of upcoming plans from friends and friends-of-friends
  • Float soft proposals to a chosen audience before they're real plans
  • Per-plan chat, polls, reactions, and host transfer
  • Squads — make crews and target plans at them
  • Pings — one-tap "anyone bored?" broadcasts
  • Live-now strip when friends mark themselves available right now
